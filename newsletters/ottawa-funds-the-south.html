Ottawa Funds Study for the South Corridor. What About the Rest?

Ottawa has committed $500,000 to study passenger rail for a portion of the south end of the E&N corridor, from Victoria West to Langford. Rail makes sense where there's population density. Outside that stretch the rest of the old line sits idle, with real potential going unused.
